# Offline vocabulary hints

The dictionary-based entries in `deck_hints.json` contain Spanish definitions and
occasional examples from Spanish Wiktionary, extracted with Wiktextract and
distributed by [Kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/eswiktionary/rawdata.html).
The source dump is dated 2026-10-01, with extraction dated 2026-10-18.

This material is made available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each dictionary hint retains its original Spanish Wiktionary entry URL, displayed
in the helper. Consult the linked entry's history for its contributors. Selection
of senses, shortened example lists, and association with vocabulary cards were
performed for this project. Dictionary language has variable difficulty.

The source entry pattern is `https://es.wiktionary.org/wiki/HEADWORD`.

Wiktextract reference: Tatu Ylonen, “Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable
Structured Data,” LREC 2022, pages 1317–1325.

Hints labelled “Español B1” were prepared for this project. The study add-on has
no machine-learning runtime dependency. A public MIT-licensed multilingual E5
model was used only during local preparation to help select dictionary senses;
neither the model nor its Python dependencies are shipped in the add-on.
